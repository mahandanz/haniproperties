Living in Klang

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices in Selangor's royal town, from a negotiator based next door in Shah Alam.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Klang area guide

Klang is Selangor's royal town and the gateway to Port Klang, the country's main seaport. Around the old town centre on the Klang River sit large newer townships such as Bandar Botanic, Bandar Bukit Tinggi, Bandar Bukit Raja and Kota Bayuemas. Prices are generally lower than in Shah Alam or Petaling Jaya, landed homes are easier to find, and the LRT3 has made the commute into Shah Alam and PJ much simpler.

Getting around

The LRT3 Shah Alam Line opened on 29 June 2026 and runs through Klang to its terminus at Johan Setia. Klang stations include Bandar Baru Klang, Pasar Klang, Jalan Meru, Jambatan Kota, Seri Andalas, Klang Jaya, Bandar Bukit Tinggi and Johan Setia, and several of them have Park n Ride. Trains run through Shah Alam to Glenmarie, for the Kelana Jaya Line, and Bandar Utama, for the MRT Kajang Line.

The KTM Port Klang Line also serves Klang town and stations towards Port Klang. For drivers, the Federal Highway and NKVE head towards Shah Alam and KL, KESAS and SKVE serve the southern townships, and the West Coast Expressway (WCE) runs up the coast towards Kapar.

Neighbourhoods

Klang town and Bandar Baru Klang

The older heart of Klang, with Little India, the riverside town centre, KPJ Klang Specialist Hospital and LRT3 stations at Bandar Baru Klang and Pasar Klang. Mostly older apartments, shophouses and established landed homes.

Bandar Botanic and Bandar Bukit Tinggi

Southern Klang townships around AEON Bukit Tinggi and GM Klang Wholesale City. Popular with families for terrace and semi-detached homes, with an LRT3 station at Bandar Bukit Tinggi.

Kota Bayuemas and Johan Setia

Newer developments in the south, close to the LRT3 terminus at Johan Setia and its large Park n Ride. A mix of serviced residences, condominiums and newer terrace homes.

Bandar Bukit Raja and Meru

Northern Klang, bordering Setia Alam, with AEON Mall Bukit Raja and quick NKVE access. Good for people who work in Shah Alam or the Bukit Raja area.

Kapar

North-west Klang along the WCE. Some of the most affordable landed homes in the area, suited to families who are happy to drive.

Pandamaran, Port Klang and Pulau Indah

The port side of Klang, close to Westport and Northport. Affordable apartments and terraces, convenient for port and logistics workers.

Daily life

  • Shopping: AEON Bukit Tinggi, AEON Mall Bukit Raja, GM Klang Wholesale City and Klang Parade.
  • Healthcare: Hospital Tengku Ampuan Rahimah (Klang's general hospital), KPJ Klang Specialist Hospital and other private hospitals around Bukit Tinggi.
  • Food: Klang is famous for bak kut teh, and Little India around Jalan Tengku Kelana is busy year-round.
  • Work: Port Klang, Westport and Northport, plus the logistics and industrial areas around them.

Who Klang suits

  • Families who want landed homes at lower prices than Shah Alam or PJ.
  • Port and logistics workers based around Port Klang, Westport or Northport.
  • Commuters heading to Shah Alam or PJ, now easier with the LRT3.
  • Buyers and investors looking for freehold landed and apartment options at accessible prices.

Common questions

How much is rent in Klang?

From our October 2026 listings, 3-bedroom apartments are mostly RM1,000 to RM1,800 a month and terrace houses RM1,200 to RM2,300.

Is Klang connected by LRT?

Yes. The LRT3 Shah Alam Line opened on 29 June 2026 and runs through Klang to Johan Setia, with stations including Bandar Baru Klang, Pasar Klang, Jambatan Kota, Bandar Bukit Tinggi and Johan Setia. The KTM Port Klang Line also serves Klang town.

Which part of Klang is best for families?

Bandar Botanic, Bandar Bukit Tinggi, Kota Bayuemas and Bandar Bukit Raja, for landed homes, schools and malls nearby.

Is Klang cheaper than Shah Alam?

Generally yes, especially for landed homes. Terrace houses in our Klang listings start from around RM370k, compared with around RM600k in our Shah Alam listings.
