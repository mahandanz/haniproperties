Living in Seri Kembangan

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices between Kuala Lumpur, Puchong and Putrajaya.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Seri Kembangan area guide

Seri Kembangan sits in the Serdang area, south of Kuala Lumpur and between Puchong, Cheras and Putrajaya. It covers established neighbourhoods around Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) and The Mines, as well as townships such as Taman Equine, Bandar Putra Permai and Puncak Jalil. The MRT Putrajaya Line and the KTM give it good rail links for the price, which makes it popular with students, hospital staff and young families.

Getting around

The MRT Putrajaya Line runs through Seri Kembangan, with stations including Serdang Jaya, UPM, Taman Equine, Putra Permai and 16 Sierra. Northbound trains go into KL, and southbound to Cyberjaya and Putrajaya. KTM Serdang station is on the KTM Seremban Line.

For drivers, the MEX and Besraya link Seri Kembangan to KL, SKVE gives an east–west route towards Kajang and Puchong, and the PLUS North–South Expressway passes along its edge.

Neighbourhoods

Serdang and Taman Universiti

The area around UPM and Hospital Serdang. Apartments and flats here are popular with students, lecturers and hospital staff.

Taman Equine and Serdang Perdana

Established neighbourhoods with their own MRT station, plenty of shops and a mix of apartments, condominiums and terraces.

Bandar Putra Permai and Lestari Perdana

Southern Seri Kembangan with many affordable apartments and some landed homes, close to the Putra Permai MRT station.

Puncak Jalil

A planned township between Seri Kembangan and Bukit Jalil, with terraces, townhouses and condominiums.

The Mines and Balakong

Towards the north-east, around The Mines and the Balakong industrial area, with older apartments and landed homes.

Daily life

  • Shopping: The Mines, plus IOI City Mall a short drive south.
  • Healthcare: Hospital Serdang, a major public hospital known for heart care.
  • Education: Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), plus national and private schools across the townships.
  • Work: UPM, Hospital Serdang, the Balakong industrial area, and quick reach of Cyberjaya, Putrajaya and KL.

Who Seri Kembangan suits

  • Students and staff at UPM or Hospital Serdang.
  • MRT commuters heading into KL or towards Cyberjaya and Putrajaya.
  • Young families looking for affordable homes close to KL.
  • Investors targeting steady student and hospital rental demand.

Common questions

How much is rent in Seri Kembangan?

From our October 2026 listings, most condominiums and serviced residences are RM1,500 to RM2,800 a month, with 3-bedroom apartments from around RM1,100.

Is Seri Kembangan connected by MRT?

Yes. The MRT Putrajaya Line runs through Seri Kembangan, with stations including Serdang Jaya, UPM, Taman Equine and Putra Permai. KTM Serdang station is on the KTM Seremban Line.

Is Seri Kembangan good for UPM students?

Yes. Serdang, Taman Universiti and Taman Equine are close to UPM, with affordable apartments and an MRT station at UPM.
