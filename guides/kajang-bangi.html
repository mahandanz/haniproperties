Living in Kajang & Bangi

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices in the south-east Klang Valley's biggest growth area.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Kajang & Bangi area guide

Kajang and Bangi sit in the south-east of the Klang Valley, between Cheras, Semenyih and Putrajaya. Kajang is an old town famous for its satay, now ringed by large townships such as Kajang 2, Bandar Sungai Long and Bangi Avenue, while Bangi grew around UKM and Bandar Baru Bangi. The MRT terminus in Kajang, several universities and prices well below KL keep demand strong from students, young families and first-time buyers.

Getting around

Kajang station is the southern end of the MRT Kajang Line, which runs through Cheras into central KL, and it is also on the KTM Seremban Line. Bangi has its own KTM station, and the Stadium Kajang MRT station serves the town centre.

For drivers, SILK loops around Kajang, the Cheras–Kajang Highway heads north into KL, LEKAS goes south towards Semenyih and Seremban, and the PLUS North–South Expressway has interchanges at Kajang and Bangi.

Neighbourhoods

Kajang town

The older centre around the MRT and KTM stations and the famous satay restaurants. Mostly older shophouses, apartments and established landed homes.

Kajang 2 and Bandar Teknologi Kajang

A large, fast-growing area with many newer condominiums and serviced residences, popular with young families and first-time buyers.

Bandar Sungai Long

Bordering Cheras, with UTAR's Sungai Long campus, a golf course and a mix of condominiums and landed homes.

Bangi Avenue and Jalan Reko

Newer high-rise projects between Kajang and Bangi, close to UKM and UNITEN. Strong student and young professional demand.

Bandar Baru Bangi and Bandar Puteri Bangi

Established Bangi townships around UKM and the Bangi commercial centres, with plenty of terrace homes.

Southville City

A Mah Sing township on the Bangi side with serviced residences and landed homes, convenient for the PLUS highway.

Daily life

  • Shopping: Bangi Gateway, Evo Mall, Bangi Sentral and the commercial centres around Kajang town and Sungai Long.
  • Healthcare: Hospital Kajang plus private hospitals in Kajang and Bangi.
  • Education: Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), UNITEN and UTAR Sungai Long, plus many national and private schools.
  • Food: Kajang satay, and the busy food scenes around Bangi and Sungai Long.

Who Kajang & Bangi suits

  • Students and university staff at UKM, UNITEN or UTAR.
  • First-time buyers looking for new condominiums and terraces well below KL prices.
  • Young families who want newer townships with schools and malls.
  • MRT commuters heading into Cheras and KL.

Common questions

How much is rent in Kajang and Bangi?

From our October 2026 listings, 3-bedroom apartments are about RM1,000 to RM1,600 a month, and most condominiums and serviced residences are RM1,300 to RM2,400.

Is Kajang connected by MRT?

Yes. Kajang is the southern end of the MRT Kajang Line, which runs through Cheras into central KL. Kajang and Bangi are also on the KTM Seremban Line.

Which part is best for students?

Bangi Avenue, Jalan Reko and Bandar Baru Bangi suit UKM and UNITEN students, while Bandar Sungai Long suits UTAR students.

Is Kajang good for first-time buyers?

Yes. Most condominiums and serviced residences in our Kajang and Bangi listings are RM280k to RM420k, and most terrace houses are RM440k to RM780k.
