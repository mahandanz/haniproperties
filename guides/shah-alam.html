Living in Shah Alam

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices in Selangor's state capital, from a negotiator based in Shah Alam.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Guides›Shah Alam

Shah Alam is Selangor's state capital: a planned city of numbered seksyens, wide roads, lakes and parks. It is home to UiTM's main campus, several other universities, the state government offices and a large industrial base. That mix keeps rental demand steady from students, young workers and families, while buyers are drawn by prices that are generally lower than Petaling Jaya or Subang Jaya.

Getting around

The biggest recent change is rail. The LRT3 Shah Alam Line opened on 29 June 2026, running from Bandar Utama to Johan Setia in Klang. Stops in Shah Alam include Stadium Shah Alam and UiTM Shah Alam. You can change to the Kelana Jaya Line at Glenmarie and to the MRT Kajang Line at Bandar Utama, which makes commuting to KL and PJ without a car realistic from many parts of Shah Alam.

The KTM Port Klang Line also runs through the city, with stations at Padang Jawa, Shah Alam and Batu Tiga. For drivers, the Federal Highway and NKVE cover the KL and Klang direction, KESAS and ELITE serve the southern seksyens, and the Guthrie Corridor Expressway (GCE) links the northern townships.

Neighbourhoods

Seksyen 7 and i-City

Next to UiTM, with the LRT station and Central i-City Mall close by. Mostly apartments, flats and serviced residences. Popular with students, lecturers and young working adults who want to walk or take the train.

Seksyen 13

Around Stadium Shah Alam, AEON Mall Shah Alam and MSU. A mix of apartments, condominiums and serviced residences, with quick access to the Federal Highway and the Stadium Shah Alam LRT station.

Seksyen 14 (city centre)

The original city centre, near Plaza Alam Sentral, SACC Mall and the state government buildings. Serviced apartments and studios here suit people working in the centre.

Seksyen 16 and Padang Jawa

Close to KTM Padang Jawa and the Federal Highway, with apartments, SOFO units and terrace houses. Usually more affordable, and convenient for both UiTM and the Klang direction.

Bukit Jelutong and Denai Alam

Northern Shah Alam townships with more landed homes, green spaces and newer developments, many on freehold land. Well suited to families, with access via the GCE and NKVE.

Kota Kemuning and the Seksyen 30s

The southern side of Shah Alam, near Bukit Rimau, Gamuda Walk and Columbia Asia Hospital. Mostly terrace and semi-detached homes with KESAS and LKSA access. A common choice for families who work towards Klang, Puchong or Subang.

Daily life

  • Shopping: AEON Mall Shah Alam, Central i-City, Plaza Alam Sentral, SACC Mall, and Setia City Mall on the Setia Alam border.
  • Healthcare: Hospital Shah Alam, KPJ Shah Alam Specialist Hospital, Avisena Specialist Hospital and Columbia Asia Bukit Rimau.
  • Education: UiTM Shah Alam, MSU and UNISEL, plus plenty of national schools in every seksyen.
  • Landmarks and outdoors: the Sultan Salahuddin Abdul Aziz Shah Mosque (Blue Mosque), Taman Tasik Shah Alam, and i-City.

Who Shah Alam suits

  • Students and university staff who want to be near UiTM, MSU or UNISEL.
  • Young workers commuting to PJ or KL, now easier with the LRT3.
  • Families looking for landed homes at lower prices than PJ or Subang.
  • Investors targeting steady student and worker rental demand.

Common questions

How much is rent in Shah Alam?

From our October 2026 listings, 3-bedroom apartments are mostly RM1,100 to RM1,650 a month, serviced residences RM1,500 to RM2,600, studios and SOHO units RM1,000 to RM1,700, and terrace houses RM1,700 to RM2,400.

Is Shah Alam connected by LRT?

Yes. The LRT3 Shah Alam Line opened on 29 June 2026, with stops including Stadium Shah Alam and UiTM Shah Alam. It connects to the Kelana Jaya Line at Glenmarie and the MRT Kajang Line at Bandar Utama. KTM stations at Padang Jawa, Shah Alam and Batu Tiga also serve the area.

Which part of Shah Alam is best for students?

Seksyen 7 and the i-City area, for walking distance to UiTM, the LRT and Central i-City Mall. Seksyen 13 suits MSU students.

Which part of Shah Alam is best for families?

Bukit Jelutong, Denai Alam, Kota Kemuning and the Seksyen 30s, for landed homes, schools and quieter streets.
