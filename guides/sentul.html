Living in Sentul

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices just north of the KL city centre.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Sentul area guide

Sentul is just north of the KL city centre, once a railway town and now one of the city's most interesting mixes of old and new. Old Sentul around Jalan Sentul Pasar keeps its shophouses and local food, while YTL's Sentul East and Sentul West have brought modern condominiums around Sentul Park and the Kuala Lumpur Performing Arts Centre (KLPac). Three rail lines and short drives to the centre make it a good-value base for city workers.

Getting around

The LRT Ampang Line ends at Sentul Timur, with Sentul station one stop south, running into the city centre. The MRT Putrajaya Line stops at Sentul Barat, and KTM Komuter stations at Sentul and Batu Kentonmen add another route.

For drivers, DUKE gives a quick route into KLCC and across to Ampang and Setapak, while MRR2 and Jalan Ipoh run north and south.

Neighbourhoods

Sentul East

YTL's development around the LRT Sentul Timur station, with modern condominiums and serviced residences.

Sentul West and Sentul Park

Condominiums around Sentul Park and KLPac, the greener and more upscale side of Sentul.

Old Sentul and Jalan Sentul Pasar

The original railway town, with shophouses, older apartments and some of the best local food in the area.

Jalan Pelangi and Kampung Batu Muda

Newer condominiums and affordable apartments on the northern side, close to Jalan Ipoh and MRR2.

Daily life

  • Shopping: Local shops in old Sentul and Sentul East, with KLCC, Sogo and the Jalan Ipoh malls a short ride away.
  • Healthcare: Hospital Kuala Lumpur and private hospitals a short drive south.
  • Culture: KLPac and Sentul Park, with regular shows and events.
  • Food: Old Sentul's long-running coffee shops and food stalls.

Who Sentul suits

  • City workers who want a short commute to KLCC and the centre.
  • Rail commuters with LRT, MRT and KTM options.
  • Young professionals looking for modern condominiums below city-centre prices.
  • Investors targeting rental demand close to the city.

Common questions

How much is rent in Sentul?

From our October 2026 listings, studios and SOHO units are about RM1,360 to RM1,960 a month and condominiums and serviced residences RM1,600 to RM2,350.

Is Sentul connected by LRT and MRT?

Yes. The LRT Ampang Line ends at Sentul Timur, the MRT Putrajaya Line stops at Sentul Barat, and KTM Komuter stops at Sentul and Batu Kentonmen.

How far is Sentul from KLCC?

Just north of the city centre. KLCC is usually a short drive via DUKE, or a quick train ride.
