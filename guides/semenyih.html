Living in Semenyih

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices in one of the most affordable townships south of Kajang.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Semenyih area guide

Semenyih is a growing town in the Hulu Langat district, just south of Kajang. Once a quiet rural town, it now has large townships such as Setia EcoHill, Bandar Rinching and Diamond City, plus the University of Nottingham Malaysia campus. Prices are among the lowest in the south-east Klang Valley, which attracts first-time buyers, students and families who are happy to drive.

Getting around

Semenyih is a driving town. The LEKAS highway runs through it, linking north to Kajang and south towards Seremban, and the PLUS North–South Expressway and SILK are close by.

There is no rail station in Semenyih. The nearest are the Kajang MRT and KTM stations, a short drive north, so many residents drive or take a bus to Kajang and catch the MRT into KL.

Neighbourhoods

Setia EcoHill

A large S P Setia township with landed homes, apartments, parks and EcoHill Walk mall. One of the most popular parts of Semenyih for families.

Bandar Rinching

A township close to the University of Nottingham Malaysia, with condominiums and landed homes that suit students and staff.

Diamond City and Sri Tanjung Kesuma

Newer areas with affordable apartments and serviced residences, among the lowest rents in the area.

Semenyih town

The original town centre and older neighbourhoods such as Taman Semenyih Mewah and Taman Tasik Semenyih, with terraces and some bungalows.

Bandar Teknologi Kajang

On the Kajang–Semenyih border, with serviced residences such as Simfoni, close to the Kajang stations and Hospital Kajang.

Daily life

  • Shopping: EcoHill Walk, Lotus's Semenyih, Econsave and Mydin, with the Kajang malls a short drive away.
  • Healthcare: Local clinics in Semenyih, with Hospital Kajang and private hospitals in Kajang nearby.
  • Education: University of Nottingham Malaysia, plus national schools in each township.
  • Outdoors: Broga Hill and the Semenyih dam area are popular for hiking and weekend trips.

Who Semenyih suits

  • First-time buyers looking for some of the lowest prices in the south-east Klang Valley.
  • Students and staff at the University of Nottingham Malaysia.
  • Families who want newer townships with parks and space.
  • Kajang commuters who drive to the Kajang MRT and KTM stations.

Common questions

How much is rent in Semenyih?

From our October 2026 listings, 3-bedroom apartments are about RM850 to RM1,400 a month and serviced residences RM1,000 to RM1,300.

Is there an MRT in Semenyih?

No. The nearest rail stations are the MRT and KTM stations in Kajang, a short drive north.

Is Semenyih good for first-time buyers?

Yes. It has some of the lowest prices in the south-east Klang Valley, with serviced residences around RM275k to RM290k in our listings.
