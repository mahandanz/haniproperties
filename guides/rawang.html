Living in Rawang

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices on the northern edge of the Klang Valley.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Rawang area guide

Rawang is a fast-growing town on the northern edge of the Klang Valley, along the North–South Expressway. Around the old town have grown large townships such as Bandar Tasik Puteri, Kota Emerald and Bukit Sentosa, with Serendah and Batang Kali further north. Landed homes here are among the most affordable within commuting distance of KL, which draws first-time buyers, young families and workers from the nearby industrial parks.

Getting around

Rawang station is on the KTM Komuter line, with trains south through Sungai Buloh and Kepong into KL, and further stations at Kuang, Serendah and Batang Kali. At Sungai Buloh you can change to the MRT.

For drivers, the PLUS North–South Expressway has interchanges at Rawang and Bukit Beruntung, while the LATAR Expressway and the Guthrie Corridor Expressway (GCE) link west towards Sungai Buloh, Puncak Alam and Shah Alam.

Neighbourhoods

Rawang town and Kota Emerald

The town centre around the KTM station, AEON Mall Rawang and Lotus's, with newer landed precincts in Kota Emerald nearby.

Bandar Tasik Puteri

A large township with terraces, bungalows and apartments, its own commercial centres and schools. One of the most popular choices for families in Rawang.

Bukit Sentosa and Bukit Beruntung

Townships north of Rawang near the industrial parks, with some of the lowest landed and apartment prices in the area.

Serendah and Batang Kali

Further north in Hulu Selangor, each with its own KTM station. Quiet, affordable terrace homes and land.

Kuang and Bandar Sungai Buaya

Smaller towns on the edges of Rawang, with affordable terraces and apartments for people who don't mind a longer drive.

Daily life

  • Shopping: AEON Mall Rawang, Lotus's Rawang and the commercial centres in Bandar Tasik Puteri.
  • Healthcare: Clinics and private hospitals in Rawang town, with Hospital Sungai Buloh to the south.
  • Work: The industrial parks around Rawang, Bukit Beruntung and Serendah, plus KL by KTM.
  • Outdoors: Templer Park and the hills and waterfalls around Rawang and Batang Kali.

Who Rawang suits

  • First-time buyers looking for landed homes at some of the lowest prices within reach of KL.
  • Young families who want space, schools and a mall nearby.
  • Factory and industrial workers based in the parks around Rawang and Serendah.
  • KTM commuters heading into Kepong or KL.

Common questions

How much is rent in Rawang?

From our October 2026 listings, terrace houses are about RM950 to RM1,600 a month and condominiums around RM1,300.

Is Rawang connected by train?

Yes. Rawang is on the KTM Komuter line into KL, with a change to the MRT possible at Sungai Buloh.

Is Rawang good for first-time buyers?

Yes. Terrace houses in our Rawang listings are about RM280k to RM450k, among the lowest prices for landed homes within commuting distance of KL.
