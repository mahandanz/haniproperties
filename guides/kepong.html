Living in Kepong

Neighbourhoods, transport, amenities and typical prices in north-west Kuala Lumpur.

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026
Hani Properties›Kepong area guide

Kepong is in north-west Kuala Lumpur, between Segambut, Selayang and the Damansara corridor. It mixes busy older neighbourhoods such as Kepong Baru and Menjalara with the master-planned Desa ParkCity, and has green spaces such as FRIM and Kepong Metropolitan Park on its doorstep. The MRT Putrajaya Line has made it much easier to reach the city, so it suits families and commuters looking for value close to KL.

Getting around

The MRT Putrajaya Line runs through Kepong, with stations including Sri Damansara Barat, Sri Damansara Sentral, Metro Prima, Kepong Baru and Jinjang, continuing into central KL and south to Putrajaya. KTM Komuter stations at Kepong and Kepong Sentral add another route into the city.

For drivers, MRR2 is the main road through Kepong, linking to Selayang and Damansara, while DUKE heads into central KL and LATAR goes north towards Rawang.

Neighbourhoods

Kepong Baru and Jalan Kepong

The busy older heart of Kepong, with shophouses, terraces, apartments and newer condominiums near the MRT.

Menjalara and Kepong Sentral

Established neighbourhoods with condominiums and serviced residences close to both MRT and KTM stations.

Desa ParkCity

A master-planned township around a large central park and lake, with upscale condominiums, landed homes and international schools. The premium end of the area.

Taman KIP and Sri Damansara

On the Selangor side towards Damansara, with affordable apartments and condominiums near the Sri Damansara MRT stations.

Desa Aman Puri

Affordable apartments close to MRR2 and the MRT, popular with first-time renters.

Daily life

  • Shopping: AEON Mall Metro Prima, Brem Mall and The Waterfront at Desa ParkCity.
  • Healthcare: Private hospitals in Kepong and Desa ParkCity, with Hospital Selayang nearby.
  • Education: International schools in Desa ParkCity, plus many national and Chinese schools across Kepong.
  • Outdoors: FRIM (Forest Research Institute Malaysia), Kepong Metropolitan Park and the Desa ParkCity central park.

Who Kepong suits

  • Families looking for established neighbourhoods, schools and parks.
  • MRT commuters heading into central KL.
  • Buyers looking for value close to KL and Damansara.
  • Upgraders eyeing Desa ParkCity's lifestyle.

Common questions

How much is rent in Kepong?

From our October 2026 listings, apartments are about RM900 to RM1,650 a month and condominiums and serviced residences RM1,350 to RM2,800.

Is Kepong connected by MRT?

Yes. The MRT Putrajaya Line runs through Kepong with stations including Metro Prima, Kepong Baru and the Sri Damansara stations, and KTM Komuter stops at Kepong and Kepong Sentral.

Is Desa ParkCity part of Kepong?

Yes. Desa ParkCity is a master-planned township on the Kepong side of north-west KL, known for its central park, lake and international schools.
