Debt Service Ratio (DSR) is calculated on a net-income basis (income after EPF, SOCSO, and tax deductions), matching how most Malaysian banks stress-test affordability. Most banks work to an internal DSR ceiling of roughly 60–70%, but the exact threshold, and what counts as a "commitment," varies by bank, income type, and credit profile. This is an estimate for discussion purposes only — always confirm actual affordability with the bank or a licensed loan consultant.

Deposit amounts (typically 2 months security + 0.5 month utility, though this varies by landlord and property type) are negotiated between landlord and tenant, not fixed by law. Tenancy agreement stamp duty is calculated per LHDN's schedule: exempt where annual rental is RM2,400 or below; otherwise RM1 for every RM250 (or part thereof) of annual rental above RM2,400 for agreements of 3 years or less, and RM2 per RM250 for longer agreements. Figures are estimates for discussion purposes only — confirm exact stamping cost via LHDN's STAMPS portal or your appointed lawyer.

Deposit is typically 10% of the reserve price (or a stated minimum, whichever is higher) and is payable immediately upon being declared the successful bidder — the exact rate and minimum vary by auctioneer/bank, so always confirm against the actual Proclamation of Sale. The balance purchase price is usually due within 90 days of the auction, extendable by a further period (commonly 30 days) subject to interest on the outstanding balance — extension terms and rates vary by bank and are set out in the Conditions of Sale. MOT stamp duty and legal fees follow the same statutory schedules as a normal purchase; the Budget 2026 first-time buyer exemption is applied where selected but auction-property eligibility should be verified with your lawyer. Margin of financing for auctioned properties is often capped lower and subject to bank valuation, which may differ from the winning bid. Figures are estimates for discussion purposes only.
