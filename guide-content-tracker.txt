## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

1. Before writing a new article, scan "PUBLISHED" for title/topic overlap —
   not just exact title matches, but overlapping subject matter (e.g. don't
   write a second "how to check tenure" article under a different title).
2. Pick the next unchecked roadmap item, or a new topic if none fit.
3. After publishing, move the item from ROADMAP to PUBLISHED with its real
   filename and date, and check the box.
4. If a completely new topic comes up that isn't on the roadmap, add it
   under the right pillar's roadmap list first, then immediately move it
   to PUBLISHED once written — keeps the list from drifting out of sync.
